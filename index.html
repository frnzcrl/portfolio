02 / DESIGN & TECHNICAL THINKING

A designer’s eye. A developer’s foundation.

AI & Automation experience

  • AI drafting & research Use ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini to turn supplied notes into client updates, summarize documents, outline presentations, and structure website copy—with source checks and human review.
  • Connected workflows Use Zapier, Make, and n8n to connect form submissions to records, task assignments, approval steps, and team notifications.
  • CRM & follow-up Use GoHighLevel to organize enquiry pipelines, capture lead details, and set up follow-up workflows with clear ownership.
  • Intake & reporting Use Google Forms and Google Sheets to collect requests, validate required fields, organize records, and prepare status summaries.
  • Business documents Use Microsoft 365 to prepare editable Word documents, reusable PowerPoint templates, and structured Excel trackers for team handoffs.
  • Custom workflow logic Use Python and JavaScript to clean and transform data, validate inputs, and add custom logic when a workflow needs more than standard integrations.

Web Designer experience

  • CMS & landing pages Use WordPress, Elementor, and Webflow to build and update pages, publish approved product information, and organize downloadable documents.
  • Storefronts & business sites Use Shopify for product pages and storefront layouts, and Wix or Squarespace for service websites and campaign pages.
  • Layouts & visual systems Use Figma to plan responsive page layouts, reusable components, typography, and clear diagrams that explain technical concepts.
  • Presentations & documents Use PowerPoint and Word to create editable pitch decks, company presentations, proposals, reports, and reusable templates with consistent spacing and typography.
  • Responsive implementation Use HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, and Bootstrap to build responsive layouts, interactive elements, accessible forms, and navigation for desktop and mobile.
  • Components & delivery Use React and Next.js for component-based website interfaces, and Git to track revisions and organize source files for handover.
  • Website quality checks Test forms and supported integrations, check links, optimize images, and resolve routine layout and formatting issues before publishing.
