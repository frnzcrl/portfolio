# Support issue triage — representative portfolio SOP

Author: Frenz Carl Asibuque
Sample only. This process must be adapted and approved before operational use.

## Purpose
Collect enough context for a useful technical escalation while keeping ownership and the next update visible.

## Scope
Software support reports needing investigation. Security incidents, outages, and urgent impact follow the team's established incident process immediately.

## 1. Review the incoming report
- Read the customer's description and existing ticket history.
- Check for related tickets before creating a duplicate.
- Record product, workspace, reporter, and first-observed time when available.
- Keep credentials and unnecessary personal information out of the ticket.

## 2. Clarify the symptom
- What was the person trying to do?
- What steps led to the issue?
- What did they expect, and what actually happened?
- Which browser, device, or version was involved?
- How many people are affected? Is a workaround available?

## 3. Capture reviewable evidence
- Use approved test access and test data.
- Document reproduction attempts and results, including unsuccessful attempts.
- Link screenshots or recordings with sensitive content redacted.
- Separate reported information, observed behavior, and hypotheses.

## 4. Apply the team's triage rules
- Assess impact and urgency using the agreed priority matrix.
- Assign the next owner; do not leave a ticket without a responsible team.
- Escalate through the established channel when the incident criteria apply.

## 5. Prepare the handoff
Include summary, environment, steps, expected/observed behavior, evidence links, impact, open questions, and requested action.

## 6. Keep the loop closed
- Record the next update or review point.
- Draft factual customer updates following the team's approval process.
- Track requested information and follow up with the appropriate owner.
- Mark the issue resolved only when the team's validation criteria are met.

## Completion checklist
- [ ] Issue context is readable without searching a chat thread.
- [ ] Evidence and unknowns are explicit.
- [ ] Impact and priority have been assessed through the agreed process.
- [ ] A next owner and next update are visible.
- [ ] No unsupported cause, fix, or deadline has been promised.
