# Website handover checklist — representative portfolio sample

Prepared by Frenz Carl Asibuque. Example checklist, not a completed client sign-off.

## Content and purpose
- [ ] Audience, page purpose, and primary action are clear.
- [ ] Headings, copy, contact links, and download links are reviewed.
- [ ] Claims and project descriptions reflect actual work.
- [ ] Image permissions and brand asset requirements are checked.

## Layout and interaction
- [ ] Review desktop, tablet, and mobile layouts.
- [ ] Confirm readable text, consistent spacing, and no horizontal overflow.
- [ ] Verify menu, buttons, forms, and interactive components.
- [ ] Check keyboard access and visible focus states.
- [ ] Provide reduced-motion behavior for animated elements.
- [ ] Ensure decorative visuals do not obscure content.

## Forms and integrations
- [ ] Identify which interactions are local demos and which use a backend.
- [ ] Validate inputs and handle errors.
- [ ] Agree on destination, owner, access, and retention for submissions.
- [ ] Keep credentials out of browser code.

## Deployment
- [ ] Agree on hosting, URL, and intended audience before publishing.
- [ ] Verify relative asset paths and production links.
- [ ] Confirm the deployed build matches the approved source.
- [ ] Keep a versioned source copy and document the recovery path.

## Handover
- [ ] Supply source files and editing instructions.
- [ ] Explain how content, assets, and interactions are updated.
- [ ] Record dependencies, fonts, and third-party assets.
- [ ] Agree on future maintenance and who owns the hosting account.
